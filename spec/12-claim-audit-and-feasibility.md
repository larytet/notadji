# 12 - Claim Audit and Feasibility Pass

Status: DRAFT (verification pass). Purpose: every statement in the specification is either re-computed, tied to a source, or labelled as an
estimate with the route that will prove it; and the whole design is checked for feasibility. Written after the review comment that the spec must not
need line-by-line correction by its reader.

## 1. Method

1. **Recompute.** `scripts/audit_numbers.py` recomputes every derived number from first principles and fails if the text disagrees; the number of checks is printed by the script. Run it before every commit.
2. **Source.** External facts (standards, silicon, regulation, patents) were looked up; each is in section 4 with its source and a status.
   Statements that could not be confirmed stay marked `[not verified]` or `[E]`.
3. **Classify.** Every requirement carries an evidence class (section 7, generated by `scripts/claim_register.py`): C computed, S sourced,
   E engineering target, P process/design rule, R regulatory.
4. **Feasibility.** Section 5 judges each major target as feasible, at risk, unproven or changed.

## 2. What the automated audit covers

Numerology (FFT sizes, symbol time), channel plan counts, TDD guard and uplink sizing, Doppler/ICI, sensitivity model, the link-budget tables
(downlink, uplink, TDD), range classes and rates, the latency budget sums, the power budget arithmetic (both columns), the CPU workload table,
requirement ID uniqueness and references, revision history, trace matrix currency. It does not and cannot prove engineering targets.

## 6. Top risks that change the plan if they fail

1. Latency stage values (encoder, decoder, display) - decided by phase A and B measurements, not by more analysis.
2. PA efficiency and linearity at 30 dBm - needs the PA study before the power class table is frozen.
3. Split-band uplink isolation (RF-030, 70 dB unproven) and regional availability of the second band.
4. Patent P-1 (antenna-aware DPD) and the unread claims of P-3 and P-4 - counsel.
5. ASIC area, power and cost - unproven until a synthesis run exists.
