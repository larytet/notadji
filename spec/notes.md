Racing use case: in FPV racing the pilot's eyes are mostly focused in one place (the line ahead, the next gate), so a fixed or slowly moving high-resolution region (static centre-weighted, head or camera direction) captures most of the benefit without gaze-tracking latency; high speed makes it necessary to lock the view on that region. Main driver: the race mode (T-05 option D, SYS-009, 25 links at 5 MHz) has a small bitrate per pilot, so concentrating bits in the middle of the image is the difference between a usable and a blurry picture; at race-track ranges bitrate, not range, is the constraint.
